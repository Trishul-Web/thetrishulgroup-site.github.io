About DevDuo Software

DevDuo Software started in 2023 in Pune, India, as a two-person studio with a simple idea: make apps we would want on our own phones. Today we publish more than 25 apps and games on Google Play.

Our games bring Indian railways, metros and local trains to your phone with real signals, routes and stations. Our tools handle everyday work like PDFs, scanning, passwords and photos, quickly and privately.

DevDuo Software is part of The Trishul Group.

Private by design

Files, notes and passwords are processed and stored on your device wherever possible.

Works without internet

Core features run offline, so our apps and games work on a train or a weak network.

Built with players
