Questions

Are my cards uploaded anywhere?

No. Cards are saved in the app’s private storage on your phone. They only leave the app if you export or share them yourself. See the privacy policy.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Scanning, organising, 3D viewing and exporting all work offline.

What happens if I uninstall the app?

All saved cards are deleted with the app, because there is no cloud backup. Export any card you want to keep as a PDF or image first.

Can I print my cards?

Yes. Export front and back on an A4 PDF sheet, or as high-resolution images ready for printing.

Is a digital copy accepted as an official ID?

The app is a personal organiser. Whether a digital copy is accepted depends on the place asking for it, so carry the original when it is required.
