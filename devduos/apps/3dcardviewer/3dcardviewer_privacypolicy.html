The short version

  • Your cards and documents are processed and stored only on your device.

  • No account is needed, and the core features work without internet.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its data permanently deletes everything.

Introduction

DevDuo Software ("we," "our," or "us"), a Trishul Group company, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our 3D Card Viewer mobile application, published on Google Play as 3D ID Wallet & Card Scanner (the "Service").

1. Local Data Processing & Storage

3D Card Viewer is designed with a strict privacy-first architecture. All image processing, PDF extractions, and 3D rendering operations are performed exclusively on your device.

  • Your Documents & Images: The files, ID cards, business cards, and documents you import or scan using the app are saved locally in the app's secure internal storage directory on your device. We do not transmit, upload, or store your documents on our servers.
  • Vault Data: Your custom tags, project notes, and presentation settings are saved locally using device preferences.
  • Card details: any names, categories or labels you type for a card (for example "Credit Card" or "Boarding Pass") are stored only on your device.

2. Sensitive Documents

Many of the cards people store, such as identity cards, driving licences, bank cards and health documents, contain sensitive personal information. Because this information never leaves your device through us, we cannot see it, read it or use it for any purpose. We do not perform any text recognition or analysis of your documents on our servers.

3. Device Permissions Required

To provide core functionality, the app may request the following permissions:

  • Camera Access: Required only if you choose to scan physical cards or documents directly into the app. We do not access your camera outside of this specific user-initiated action.
  • Photo/Storage Access: Required to allow you to import existing photos or PDFs from your gallery, and to export your finished 3D creations, PDF sheets, or PNG files back to your device. On newer Android versions you can choose to share only selected photos.
  • Home screen widgets: if you add a card widget to your home screen, a preview of that card is shown on your home screen and may be visible to anyone who can see your phone. You can remove a widget at any time.

4. Information We Collect

Because the app operates entirely offline for its core features, our data collection is extremely limited:

  • Anonymous Analytics & Crash Logs: We may collect anonymous, aggregated usage data, such as app crashes and basic feature usage. This data contains absolutely no personal information or document data, and is used solely to identify bugs and improve app stability.
  • Purchase Information: If you purchase premium features, the transaction is securely handled by the respective app store (Google Play or Apple App Store). We receive anonymous tokens to verify your purchase, but we do not receive your payment details.

5. Exporting and Sharing

Your cards only leave the app when you choose to export or share them, for example as a PDF sheet or PNG image. When you share a file to another app (such as email or a messaging app), that app's own privacy policy applies to the copy you send. Please take care when sharing images of identity documents.

6. How We Use and Share Information

We use the limited information described above only to provide, maintain and improve the Service. We do not sell, rent or trade any information, and we never share your documents with advertisers or any other third party.

7. Data Security

Your imported images and PDF creations are stored securely within the app's local sandbox environment provided by the operating system (Android/iOS). However, as the data is stored locally, anyone with physical access to your unlocked device may be able to view your saved projects.

We recommend using a screen lock on your phone and removing any home screen widgets for sensitive cards.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep it in the app. You can delete it at any time:

  • Delete a card: remove any card or project from inside the app.
  • Uninstall the App: uninstalling 3D Card Viewer permanently erases all locally stored cards, projects and settings.
  • Clear App Data: go to Settings > Apps > 3D Card Viewer > Storage > Clear Data / Clear Storage.

Because we do not keep a copy on our servers, deleted data cannot be recovered by us.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Service, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support.devduos@thetrishulgroup.com
