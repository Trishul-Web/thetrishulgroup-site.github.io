Why it matters

Questions

Are my links or code sent to a server?

No. Pages are loaded and converted on your device using its built-in browser engine. We don’t log the websites you convert.

Will the PDF have a watermark?

No. Your PDFs are clean, with no watermark.

Can I still get normal A4 pages?

Web to PDF is built for seamless output, so long pages stay in one piece and nothing gets cut in half.

Where are my PDFs kept?

In the offline Vault inside the app, and wherever you choose to save or share them.
