Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 28, 2026

DevDuo's built the IndexoFit software application (formerly known as SmartBMI Tracker / BMI Calculator). This Service is provided by DevDuo's for health and wellness tracking.

This page informs visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decides to use our Service.

1. Local Processing & Storage of Personal Data

Strict Local Data Retention: IndexoFit processes personal health and fitness metrics including your age, height, weight, BMI calculations, daily water intake, meal photos, dynamic nutrition logs, and workout activity logs. All calculations and data storage happen strictly on your local device. We do not upload, transmit, store, or sell your health metrics or photos on external cloud servers.

2. Data Erasure & Uninstallation

Because all data is processed and stored locally on your device, you maintain complete ownership and control over your records. You can completely and permanently delete all your data at any time by clearing the application storage/cache in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the app from your device.

The app may utilize third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device for analytics, app functionality, or advertising purposes (e.g., ad networks or crash reporting tools). Links to privacy policies of third-party service providers used by the app:

4. Children’s Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can assist with necessary actions.
