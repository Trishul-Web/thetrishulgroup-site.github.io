The short version

  • Your photos are compressed and resized only on your device. They are never uploaded.

About This Policy

DevDuo's ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices regarding the Photo Compressor application (the "Service"), published on Google Play as Photo Compressor & Resizer.

1. Local Processing Guarantee

Photo Compressor is designed with privacy as a priority. All image compression, comparison, and batch processing occur locally on your device. We do not upload, transmit, or store your photos on any external servers.

This also applies to resizing, the No-Crop Blur Canvas, Snap & Compress, Storage Sweeper scans and ZIP export, which all run on your phone.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • Your Photos: We have absolutely no access to the images you select or compress.
  • Personal Identity: We do not require you to create an account, nor do we collect personally identifiable information such as your name or email address for core usage.
  • Location: The app does not request your location. If you turn on Remove EXIF Data, location and camera details stored inside a photo are stripped from the compressed copy.

3. Data We May Collect

To keep the app functional and bug-free, we may collect minimal, non-identifiable data:

  • Anonymous Analytics: We may gather anonymous crash reports and basic usage metrics to understand how features (like the Compare Slider or Batch Processing) are performing.

4. Device Permissions

The app asks only for permissions needed for the features you use:

  • Photos and media: to let you pick photos to compress and to save the results. On newer Android versions you can allow access to selected photos only.
  • Camera: only when you use Snap & Compress to take a new photo.
  • Internet: used for ads and purchases. Compression itself works offline.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Data Retention: Because your data (such as lifetime compression statistics and app preferences) is stored exclusively on your local device and not on any external servers, we retain this data for only as long as you keep the App installed on your device. Processed images are saved directly to your device's local gallery or storage.

Data Deletion: You have full control over your data. To completely delete all of your app data, you can do either of the following:

  • Uninstall the App: Deleting the Photo Compressor app from your device will permanently erase all locally stored app data and statistics.
  • Clear App Data: You can clear the app's data through your device's system settings (e.g., Settings > Apps > Photo Compressor > Storage > Clear Data / Clear Storage).
  • Images: Any compressed images you have chosen to save to your device's gallery must be deleted manually through your device's photos app.

6. Third-Party Services

If the app contains advertisements or utilizes third-party analytics (like Google Firebase), these services may collect anonymous identifiers as dictated by their respective privacy policies.

  • Google AdMob: may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and device information to show and measure ads. Where required by law, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.
  • Google Play Billing: PRO purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details. A purchase status service (such as RevenueCat) may receive an anonymous app user ID to confirm your PRO access.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Sharing and ZIP Export

When you share compressed photos or a ZIP file, the app opens your phone's share menu. Files are sent only to the app and people you choose.

8. Your Choices

  • Allow access only to the photos you want to compress.
  • Remove EXIF data before sharing photos.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
  • Clear app data or uninstall the app at any time.

9. Security

App data is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Because your photos are never uploaded, they are not exposed to any server-side risk from us.

10. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy occasionally. Changes will be reflected in the app and on this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
