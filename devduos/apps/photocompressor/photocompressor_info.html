Questions

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. All compression and resizing happens on your phone, and your photos never leave your device. See our privacy policy.

Can I compress to an exact KB size?

Yes. Enter a target such as 100 KB or 200 KB and the app adjusts the photo to fit.

Are my original photos changed?

Compressed copies are saved separately. Always keep a backup of important originals before batch processing.

Which formats are supported?

JPEG, JPG, PNG and WEBP, with JPG or WEBP output and ZIP export for batches.
