Questions

Can I play without internet?

Yes. Every mode, AI level and tournament works fully offline.

Is Unbeatable really unbeatable?

In Classic it plays perfectly, so the best you can do is a draw. Try the other modes for a fairer fight.

Can two people play on one phone?

Yes. Choose 2 player mode, pick avatars and names, and pass the phone between turns.

Do I need an account?

No. Your match history and settings stay on your device.
