About This Policy

DevDuo ("we," "our," or "us") operates the TTE Battle: Tic Tac Toe Arena mobile application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to player privacy, data protection, and local retention models.

Local Game Save & Data Deletion Policy

TTE Battle is engineered on a strict offline, local-first architecture. We do not collect, upload, transmit, or store your player names, tournament scores, or battle history on remote cloud servers.

Local Data Storage:
All match records, score tallies, avatar selections, and game preferences are saved strictly within your device's private sandbox storage using local persistence mechanisms.

Data Deletion Instructions:
Because your game data resides entirely on your device, you have total authority over its deletion:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete individual match records directly from the Match History screen or clear all history with a single tap.
  • Clear App Storage: Navigate to your device's Settings > Apps > TTE Battle > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data to reset all statistics.
  • Application Uninstallation: Uninstalling the application from your device permanently purges all local records and history files.
  • No Server Deletion Required: Since we do not harvest or retain your data on external servers, external web-based deletion tickets are not needed.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Personal Identity: We do not require accounts, logins, emails, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • Location & Media Data: The game does not track GPS locations or access your camera, media, or personal photos.
  • Contacts and Microphone: The game does not access your contacts or microphone.

2. Player Names and Avatars

Names you type for players in 2 player mode and the avatars you pick are used only to show who is playing and to label your match history. They stay on your device and are never sent to us.

3. Third-Party Services & Diagnostics

The app operates 100% offline. If diagnostic logs are collected via Google Play Console crash reporting, they consist solely of anonymized hardware specifications and stack traces to fix app crashes and optimize performance.

Google Play may also collect information when you download, update or rate the game, under the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Device Permissions

TTE Battle does not need any special permissions to play. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or storage-wide file access, and all game modes work without an internet connection.

5. How We Use and Share Information

Because we do not receive your game data, we do not use it for advertising and we never sell or share it with anyone.

6. Security

Your match history and settings are kept in the game's private storage on your device, protected by Android's app sandbox.

7. Children's Privacy

Our game is designed for all audiences and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected directly on this page with a revised "Last Updated" timestamp.
