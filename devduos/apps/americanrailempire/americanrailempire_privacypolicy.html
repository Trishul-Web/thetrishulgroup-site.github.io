About This Policy

DevDuo ("we," "our," or "us") operates the American Rail Empire mobile game application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy describes how player data, local saves, and third-party advertising modules operate within the game for full Google Play Store compliance.

Local-First Game Save & Data Deletion Policy

American Rail Empire runs on a strict offline, local-first architecture. We do not collect, transmit, or store personal identifiable information (such as your real name, address, or email) on remote servers.

Local Data Storage:
All game save states—including unlocked platforms, station styles, rolling stock inventory, formed train rakes, career dispatches, and in-game token balances—are saved strictly on your local device via encrypted sandboxed databases.

Data Deletion Instructions:
Because your game data is entirely on-device, you have full control over resetting or deleting it:

  • Clear App Storage: Navigate to your device's Settings > Apps > American Rail Empire > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data to wipe all progress.
  • Uninstall the App: Removing the app from your device immediately and permanently erases all local database records.
  • No Remote Server Deletion Needed: As no personal profile data is uploaded to our servers, you do not need to submit external web deletion tickets.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Account Profiles: No sign-up, email, phone number, or password is required to play.
  • Personal Media & Files: The game does not read or upload your personal media, photos, or storage files.
  • Precise Location: The game does not request or track GPS location data.
  • Contacts, Camera and Microphone: The game does not request access to your contacts, camera or microphone.

2. Information Collected Automatically

When ads are shown, the advertising provider may automatically collect limited technical information, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • device model, operating system version and language
  • approximate (coarse) location derived from your IP address
  • ad interactions, such as whether a rewarded ad was completed

This information is collected by the advertising provider to deliver ads, measure performance and prevent fraud. It is not linked by us to your identity.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To provide optional ad-rewarded game multipliers (such as 2x contract payouts and bonus spins), we use certified third-party ad networks:

  • Google AdMob: May process non-identifying diagnostic information and device advertising IDs to deliver relevant rewarded and interstitial ads. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for details on their data processing.

4. Ad Personalisation and Consent

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can also limit ad tracking at any time:

  • On Android, go to Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Turning off personalised ads means you may still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.
  • Watching rewarded ads is always optional. You can play the full game without them.

5. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Optional in-game purchases (such as the VIP Lifetime Pass and Tycoon Cash Packs) are processed exclusively by Google Play In-App Billing. DevDuo does not receive or store your credit card or financial credentials.

6. How We Use Information

We use the limited information described above only to run the game, provide optional rewards, verify purchases and fix technical problems. We do not sell your personal information.

7. Children's Privacy

American Rail Empire is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Security

Your game save is stored in the app's private storage on your device, protected by Android's app sandbox. No method of storage is completely secure, but we only keep what the game needs to run.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated timestamp.
