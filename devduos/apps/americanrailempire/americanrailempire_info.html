Questions

Can I play offline?

Yes. Your game runs and saves on your phone, and your railway keeps earning while you are away. Rewarded ads and purchases need a connection.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, email or password. Your progress is saved on your device.

Are ads required?

No. Rewarded ads are optional and give bonuses like 2x contract payouts and extra spins.

What happens to my progress if I change phones?

Progress is stored only on your current device, so uninstalling, clearing data or moving to a new phone starts a new game.
