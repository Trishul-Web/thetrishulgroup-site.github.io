INDIA'S METRO DRIVING GAME

Take the controls of the metro.

Drive metro trains through eleven Indian cities, high on viaducts above busy roads, along street-level corridors and deep in tunnels under the old city. Stop at the yellow marker, open the doors, let the passengers board and keep the line on time. Or design a whole metro network of your own.

What you can do

🏙️

Drive real city lines

Station order and levels follow the real lines of eleven Indian cities, with distances shortened for play.

🌉

Viaduct, street and tunnel

Climb onto viaducts, glide along fenced corridors and dive into tunnels, with smooth ramps between every level.

🚦

Signals and speed limits

Set your speed with simple +5 and −5 controls, obey every signal and limit, and stop right at the marker for a perfect score.

🚪

Doors and passengers

Open the doors, watch passengers get off and board, wait for the doors-closing call and depart on time.

🔀

Interchanges

Big stations where several lines meet on different floors, with other trains arriving and leaving above and below you.

🛠️

Build your own metro

Add lines and stations, choose elevated, street or underground for each one, pick colours and trains, and the route between them builds itself.

⚡

Quick route

Name two stations and start driving in seconds, one way or as an endless round with cab changes at both ends.

📢

Announcements

Next station, arrival, doors and safety calls in English and Hindi, spoken by your phone's own voice.

🌧️

Time and weather

Morning, noon, golden evenings and city nights, with clouds, rain and thunder that change as you drive.

🗺️

City maps in 3D

See every metro as a city map with parks, rivers, roads and buildings, top-down or in a tilted 3D view.

🚆

Pick your train

Choose from 32 liveries and set the train length from one coach up to eight coaches between the engines.

📤

Share your metros

Send a metro you built to friends as a file. They tap it and it opens straight in the game.
