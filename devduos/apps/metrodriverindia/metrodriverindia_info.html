Easy to drive, fun to master

Set your speed and the train does the rest. Your job is to read the signals and stop right on the mark.

  • +5Raise your set speed and the train speeds up by itself.
  • −5Slow down for curves, limits and signals.
  • Set 0Bring the train to a smooth stop.
  • STOPHalt at the yellow marker for a perfect score.
  • DoorsOpen up, let everyone board, close and depart.

Questions

Do I need an account or internet to play?

No account is needed. You can drive without internet. Ads, the Hindi language pack and optional rewarded unlocks need a connection.

Can I add my own city?

Yes. Build a metro for any city or town, add lines and stations, then drive it. You can also share it with friends as a file.

Are the lines and maps real?

Station order and levels follow the real lines, but distances are shortened for play and the maps are illustrations, not real maps.

My city is missing. Can you add it?

Write to support.devduos@thetrishulgroup.com or use Feedback & support in the game.
