Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 17, 2026

DevDuo Software ("DevDuo", "we", "our" or "us") makes the Metro Driver India mobile game (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the Game uses, where it is stored, which third-party services are involved, and how you can delete your data.

Local Data Retention & Deletion Policy

Metro Driver India saves your game on your device only. We do not run our own game servers, and we do not upload, synchronize or store your game data in the cloud. You do not need an account to play.

What is saved on your device:
Your settings (sound on or off, map view, announcement on or off, English and Hindi voice choices), the metros and routes you build in My metros (lines, stations, colours, trains and distances), Hindi station names you type or that were translated on your device, and the Hindi language pack if you download it. This is kept in the Game's private app storage on your phone.

How to delete your data:

  • Delete one metro: Open Start Driving > My metros, select the metro and tap the delete icon.
  • Clear all game data: Go to your device's Settings > Apps > Metro Driver India > Storage > Clear storage / Clear data. This permanently erases all settings and saved metros.
  • Uninstall the Game: Uninstalling permanently removes all locally saved data.
  • Feedback you sent us: Email us at the address below and we will delete your feedback messages.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No account: The Game does not ask for a name, phone number, password or login.
  • No location: The Game does not access your GPS or location. Cities and station names in the Game are built in or typed by you.
  • No camera, microphone, contacts or photos: The Game does not access them.
  • No files without your choice: The Game only opens a file when you pick it yourself with Import, or when you open a metro file in the Game from another app.

2. Feedback & Support

If you choose to send a message through the in-game Feedback & support window, we receive:

  • the topic you selected and the message you wrote,
  • your email address, only if you choose to enter it (so we can reply),
  • the Game version and your device brand, model and Android version, so we can fix problems.

The message is sent through Google Forms and stored in our Google account. We use it only to answer you, fix bugs and improve the Game. We never sell it or use it for advertising.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Metro Driver India shows ads served by Google AdMob:

  • Interstitial ads may appear at natural breaks, for example when you start a drive, end or leave a trip, save, share, import, copy, edit or delete a metro, or start building a new one. We show at most one of these every few minutes.
  • Rewarded ads are optional. You choose to watch one to unlock a feature for your current trip plan or building session, for example a train livery, a longer train, depot starts, infinite rounds, announcements, custom distances, extra lines or a quick retry after a missed station.

To show ads, AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data. It uses this to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure performance and prevent fraud. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Premium

A one-time Premium purchase is planned for a future update. When it is available, payment will be handled entirely by Google Play. We will never see or store your card or payment details; the Game will only store on your device that Premium is active.

5. Announcements & Hindi Station Names

  • Train announcements use the text-to-speech voice already on your phone. The text is spoken on your device. If a voice is missing, the Game can open your phone's voice settings so you can download it from Google.
  • Hindi station names are translated on your device using Google ML Kit. The Hindi language pack (about 30 MB) is downloaded once from Google; after that, translation works offline on your device. The station names you translate are not sent to us.

6. Sharing & Importing Metros

When you tap Share on a metro, the Game creates a .metrodriverindia file and opens your phone's share menu (for example WhatsApp). The file contains only the metro itself: its name, lines, stations, colours, trains and distances. It never contains personal information, and it is sent only when and to whom you choose. When you import a metro file, it is read on your device and saved in My metros.

7. More from DevDuo

The More from DevDuo page downloads a list of our other apps and their pictures from GitHub. Like any website, GitHub receives your IP address to deliver this list. No personal information is sent. Store links open in Google Play or your browser.

9. Security

Data the Game sends to Google services (ads, feedback, language and voice downloads) and to GitHub is transferred over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your saved game stays in the Game's private storage on your device.

10. Children's Privacy

Metro Driver India is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through the feedback form, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy
