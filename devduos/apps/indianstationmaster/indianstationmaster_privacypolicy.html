Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 27, 2026

DevDuo ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Indian Station Master mobile application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to user privacy and details our data handling and retention practices for Play Store compliance.

Local Game Save & Data Deletion Policy

Indian Station Master is designed as a local-first offline simulation game. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal identification data, station progress, or wallet tokens on remote cloud servers.

Data Storage:
All game save states (unlocked train rakes, station upgrades, ticket counter levels, retiring suite configurations, and token balances) are written strictly to an isolated local database on your device.

Data Deletion Instructions:
Because your save data resides entirely on your device, you possess total authority over its deletion:

  • Clear App Cache/Storage: Navigate to your device's Settings > Apps > Indian Station Master > Storage > Clear Data. This instantly and permanently resets all saved game states.
  • Application Uninstallation: Uninstalling the application purges the local database and all associated progress permanently.
  • No Remote Deletion Request Needed: Since no personal data is ever collected or held on our backend servers, external web-based account deletion requests are not required.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Personal Identity: No player account registration, email address, name, or phone number is required to play.
  • Location & Contact Data: The game does not track your physical location or access your device contacts.

2. Third-Party Advertising & Services

To provide this simulation game for free, we integrate third-party ad networks:

  • Google AdMob: Serves rewarded and interstitial advertisements (e.g., doubling coin collections or approving VIP passage). AdMob may utilize device identifiers (Advertising ID) and coarse diagnostic metrics according to Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Crash Reporting & Diagnostics

We may receive anonymous, non-identifiable system error logs provided through Google Play Services to diagnose rendering issues or memory performance across different Android hardware models.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this policy. Revisions will be made effective immediately by updating the "Last Updated" timestamp above.
