The short version

  • Your resumes and career data are stored on your device. No account is needed.
  • Text is sent to an AI service only when you use a cloud AI feature, and only to generate that result.
  • On-device AI models, when selected, process your text entirely on your phone.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Premium purchases are handled by Google Play.

About This Policy

DevDuo's built the ResuMate AI app as an Ad-Supported app. This SERVICE is provided by DevDuos and is intended for use as is.

This Privacy Policy explains what information ResuMateAI: Smart Resume Tools (the "app") uses, how it is processed and the choices you have.

1. Data Storage & Privacy

ResuMate AI follows a local-first principle. All resume data (names, contact info, experience, etc.) you enter is stored securely on your device using a local database. We (DevDuos) generally do not have access to this data.

This also includes your resume versions, autosave history, cover letters and messages you generate, Career Hub job tracking, interview notes, challenge progress and XP, theme choice and settings.

2. Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or provide functionality:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect data such as Advertising ID, coarse location, and usage metrics to serve relevant ads.
  • Google Gemini API: When you use AI features (e.g., "AI Writer", "ATS Scanner", "Review Resume"), the text content (resume data, job descriptions) is sent to Google's Gemini API for processing. This data is used solely to generate the response and is not used to train Google's models.

3. On-Device AI Models

The app can also offer offline AI models that you choose to download. When an on-device model is active, your resume text is processed entirely on your phone and is not sent to any AI service. Downloaded models are stored in the app's storage and can be removed in Settings.

4. PDF Import and Export

  • PDF import: when you upload an existing resume, the file is read from your device to fill in editable sections. If an AI feature is used to understand the text, that text is processed as described in section 2.
  • PDF and website export: resumes exported as PDF and portfolio websites saved as HTML files are created on your device and saved where you choose. They are shared only when you share them.

5. Device Permissions

  • Files and storage: to import a resume PDF and save exported PDFs or HTML files.
  • Internet: to use cloud AI features, show ads, process purchases and download optional AI models.
  • The app does not need access to your contacts, camera, microphone or location to build resumes.

6. Premium Purchases

Premium features are purchased through Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details. We only receive confirmation that your purchase is valid so your features can be unlocked.

7. Log Data

In a case of an error in the app, we collect data and information (through third-party products like Google Play Services) on your phone called Log Data. This may include your device IP address, device name, operating system version, and the time and date of your use of the Service.

8. Your Choices

  • Use on-device AI models when you prefer your text never to leave your phone.
  • Avoid entering details you do not want processed by a cloud AI feature, such as government ID numbers.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings. Where required by law, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.
  • Delete individual resumes inside the app at any time.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data is stored on your device, it stays there until you remove it:

  • Delete in the app: remove any resume, document or tracked job.
  • Clear app data: go to your device's Settings > Apps > ResuMateAI > Storage > Clear storage.
  • Uninstall: uninstalling the app permanently removes all locally stored data.

Exported PDF and HTML files saved to your device must be deleted separately.

10. Security

Data sent to AI services travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. Your saved data is kept in the app's private storage on your device. We recommend using a screen lock, because anyone with access to your unlocked phone could open the app.

11. Children's Privacy

ResuMateAI is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
