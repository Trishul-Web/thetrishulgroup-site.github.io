    Questions

    Will my resume pass ATS systems?

    ATS-friendly templates and the ATS scanner help you match keywords and structure. No tool can guarantee results, so always review your resume before applying.

    Where is my resume stored?

    Your resumes are saved on your device. Text is processed only when you use an AI feature. See our privacy policy for details.

    Can I import my existing resume?

    Yes. Upload a PDF and the app turns it into editable sections.

    Should I check AI-written content?

    Yes. AI can make mistakes, so always review and edit anything it writes before sending it to employers.
