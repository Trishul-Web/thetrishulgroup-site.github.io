Questions

Do I need any equipment?

No. Every routine uses only your body, so you can stretch at your desk, at home or while travelling.

How long does a session take?

Most flows take around 10 minutes. There are also quick 3 to 5 minute routines for short breaks.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Your answers, history and streaks are stored on your phone. See our privacy policy for details.

Is it suitable for beginners?

Yes. You choose beginner, intermediate or advanced during setup and the app adapts your daily flow to your level.

Can it replace physiotherapy?

No. The app is for general wellness and flexibility. If you have an injury or ongoing pain, please talk to a doctor or physiotherapist first.
