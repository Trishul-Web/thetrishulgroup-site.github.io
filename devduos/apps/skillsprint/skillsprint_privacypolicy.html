About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how DevDuo Software ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the SkillSprint mobile application (the "App"). SkillSprint is a learning app with 200+ interactive courses, audio lessons, module tests, certificates, skill cards, and an on-device AI course creator.

1. Summary

  • Your learning progress, test scores, certificates, skill cards, saved courses, and AI-generated courses are stored only on your device. We do not upload them to our servers.
  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob and processes purchases through Google Play Billing and RevenueCat. These services collect some device and usage data, described below.
  • If you send us feedback from inside the App, we receive the details you submit.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information stored only on your device

The following information is created and saved inside the App's private storage on your device. It never leaves your device unless you choose to share it yourself:

  • Course progress, completed lessons, test results, and scores.
  • Certificates and skill cards you earn, including the certificate ID and date.
  • The name you enter for your certificates. It is used only to print your name on your certificates and skill cards. You can change it anytime in Profile.
  • Saved (bookmarked) courses and app settings such as the intro video preference.
  • Courses and lectures you create with the on-device AI. The AI model runs entirely on your phone, so the topics you type and the content it generates are never sent to us.
  • Your XP, streaks and weekly challenge progress.

When you tap "Share" or "Save to Gallery", the certificate image or PDF is created on your device and shared only with the app or person you choose.

3. Information you choose to send us

If you use Send Feedback or Report Bug inside the App, the following is sent to us through Google Forms:

  • Your email address (optional, but required for payment-related requests).
  • The topic you select and the message you write.
  • The App version, your device brand and model, and Android version, to help us fix problems.

We use this information only to reply to you and improve the App. You can ask us to delete it at any time by emailing us.

5. Ad consent and your choices

  • Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later.
  • Buying Lifetime Pro removes ads.
  • You can use the App without sending any feedback and without saving any certificate name.

6. Permissions the App uses

  • Internet and network access: to load ads, check purchases, download the optional AI model, and receive update notes.
  • Photos and media (Android 10 and below only): to save your certificates and skill cards to your gallery when you tap "Save to Gallery". On newer Android versions no permission is needed.
  • Keep screen awake: only while the AI model is downloading, so the download does not stop.

The App does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location.

7. Children's privacy

SkillSprint is a general learning app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through the feedback form, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data security

Data stored by the App is kept in the private storage area that Android gives to each app. Information sent to third-party services is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Anyone with access to your unlocked device may still be able to open the App and see your progress.

9. International transfers

Google, RevenueCat, GitHub and Cloudflare may process data on servers outside your country. They apply their own safeguards as described in their privacy policies.

10. Data retention and deletion

  • On-device data: you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App. This permanently removes your progress, certificates, and skill cards.
  • Feedback messages: email us and we will delete your submissions.
  • Third-party data: data held by Google AdMob, Google Play, or RevenueCat is managed under their own policies linked above.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes. The new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

12. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data deleted, contact DevDuo Software at: support.devduos@thetrishulgroup.com
