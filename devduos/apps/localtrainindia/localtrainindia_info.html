LOCAL TRAININDIA

Drive the city's local train.

Take the controls of an Indian suburban local. Run station to station through crowded cities, stop right at the board, open the doors for the rush-hour crowd, obey every signal and keep your line moving, from the first train at dawn to the last one at night.

Get it on the Play Store

What you can do

🚈

Drive the local

Pull away with simple speed controls, run between stations and bring a 9 or 12-car rake to a precise stop at the yellow car-stop board.

🚦

Real signalling

Home, starter and automatic signals with red, yellow, double yellow and green aspects. Pass one at danger and the trip ends.

🚪

Doors and crowds

Open the doors, let passengers get off and on, close up, wait for the guard's bell and the starter to clear. Peak hour brings bigger crowds.

🏙️

Seven cities

Lines in Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Pune, with slow and fast services and one to four track sections.

🛠️

Line Studio

Build your own line: stations, distances, tracks and speed limits. Share it with friends as a file and add theirs in one tap.

🔀

Points, loops and overtakes

Take crossovers at 30 km/h, wait in the loop while another train crosses, or let a faster train overtake you.

📢

Announcements

Station and in-train announcements in your local language, Hindi and English, with station boards to match.

🌧️

Time and weather

Dawn, day, dusk and night, monsoon rain and winter haze, with headlights, tunnels, bridges and level crossings along the way.

🔁

Car shed and endless runs

Bring the empty rake out of the car shed, take it back at the end, or change cab at the terminus and keep driving back and forth.

Visual Showcase

Hand-drawn Indian suburbs with chawls, markets, high-rises, mangroves, creeks and hill cuttings, island platforms full of people, foot overbridges, car sheds and colour-light signals, with sounds made in code and background music.
