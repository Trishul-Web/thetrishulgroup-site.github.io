Questions

Do I need an account for my links page?

No. Your page details live inside the QR link itself, so there is no sign-up, no server account and no monthly fee.

Will my links page expire?

No. Because the details are stored in the QR link, your page keeps working as long as the QR exists.

Can I change my links later?

Open the code from History, edit it and create it again. Remember that people need the new QR to see the changes.

Is PRO a subscription?

No. PRO is a one-time purchase through Google Play.
