Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. Your settings and Activities are saved on your device.

What happens if I pass a red signal?

Your journey ends, just like the real rule. Watch for yellow and double yellow to slow down in time.

Can I share my own scenarios?

Yes. Create an Activity with stops, loops, crossings, weather and time, then share it with friends to import and play.

Are ads required?

Rewarded ads are optional and unlock extra features for your next journey. Premium is a one-time purchase that removes ads and unlocks everything.
