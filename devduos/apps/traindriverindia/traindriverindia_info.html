Drive India's Railways.

Plan your own route across India, build your train coach by coach, and drive it through ghats, deserts, coasts and cities. Obey the signals, stop at the board, wait in the loop for crossings, run round your loco and bring the rake home to the yard.

Download on Play Store

What you can do

🗺️

Plan your own route

Pick any start, stops and destination, add loco reversals and banker engines, and choose how long the journey should be.

🚦

Real signalling

Distant, home, starter and automatic signals with red, yellow, double yellow and green aspects. Pass one at danger and your journey ends.

🔀

Crossings & overtakes

Wait in the loop while express trains cross or overtake you, sometimes both at once, or run through while others wait for you.

🔧

Yard shunting

Place the rake with the yard shunter, bring your loco out, couple up, run round at reversal stations and stable everything at journey's end.

🚂

Build your train

Electric and diesel locos, trainsets, LHB and ICF coaches, power cars and freight wagons, all arranged the way you want.

📢

Station announcements

Platform announcements in English, Hindi and your local language, with station boards in the same languages.

🌧️

Live weather & time

Sunrise, night, monsoon storms and winter fog change as you drive across ten Indian landscapes.

🏆

Create & share activities

Design your own scenarios with loop stations and train movements, then share them with friends as a code.

Visual Showcase

Hand-painted Indian scenery with tunnels, bridges, level crossings and busy stations, head and tail lamps at night, diesel smoke, synthesised train sounds and background music.
