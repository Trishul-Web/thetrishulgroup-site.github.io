Questions

Do I need an account?

No. SaveAnything works without any sign-up. Everything is stored in a local database on your phone.

Why does the app use the internet?

Only to fetch link previews such as titles, cover images and site icons from the websites you save. Your vault is never uploaded.

Can I lock private items?

Yes. Set a master passcode, then lock individual items or whole tags so they stay hidden in your feed until unlocked.

What happens if I uninstall the app?

Your vault is deleted with the app because it lives only on your device. Keep a device backup if you want to protect your saved items.
