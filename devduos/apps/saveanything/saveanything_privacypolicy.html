The short version

  • Everything you save stays in a local database on your device. Nothing is uploaded to us.
  • No account, no cloud sign-up and no background tracking.
  • The internet is used only to fetch previews for links you save.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes your vault.

About This Policy

DevDuo ("we", "us", or "our") built the SaveAnything app. This SERVICE is provided by DevDuo and is intended for use as is. This page is used to inform users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information.

1. Data Collection and Use

SaveAnything is a 100% offline, privacy-first application. We do NOT collect, transmit, upload, or store your personal data, links, files, media, or notes on any external servers. All data you save within the app is processed and stored strictly locally on your device using a secure local database.

  • Internet Permission: The app requests internet access solely to fetch rich link previews (titles, descriptions, and thumbnails) directly from the public websites you bookmark. We do not track or log the URLs you save.
  • Storage/Media Permission: The app requests storage access to allow you to select local files and images to save into your vault. The app does not scan your storage or upload your files to the cloud.

2. Link Previews

When a link preview is fetched, your device connects directly to the website you saved (for example YouTube or a news site) to read its public title, description and images. Like any visit to a website, that website can see your IP address and basic device information. This connection is between your device and that website only; it does not pass through our servers.

3. Passcode and Locked Items

  • Your master passcode, security questions and the list of locked items or tags are stored only on your device.
  • We cannot see or reset your passcode. If you forget it, use the recovery option in the app.
  • Locked items stay on your device in the same local database and are hidden from your feed until unlocked.

4. Home Screen Widgets and Sharing

  • Widgets: if you add Quick Add, Recent Vault or Master Board widgets, recent item titles may be visible on your home screen to anyone who can see your phone.
  • Sharing into SaveAnything: when you share a link or file from another app, it is saved to your local vault only.
  • Sharing out: when you share an item, your phone's share menu sends it only to the app and people you choose.

5. Insights and Statistics

The Insights tab (totals, favourites, top sources and most used tags) is calculated on your device from your own vault. These statistics are never sent to us.

6. Data Retention Policy

Because SaveAnything is an offline application, the data you save within your vault is retained strictly locally on your specific device for as long as the application remains installed. We do not possess a centralized server, and therefore we do not hold, backup, or retain any of your data ourselves.

7. Data Deletion Policy (How to Delete Your Data)

You have complete control over your data. Since your data is not stored on our servers, we cannot delete it for you remotely. However, you can easily and permanently delete your data from the app at any time using any of the following methods:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete individual vault items (links, files, notes) directly within the SaveAnything app by tapping the options menu on any item and selecting "Delete".
  • Clear App Data: You can wipe your entire vault instantly by navigating to your device's Settings > Apps > SaveAnything > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstall the App: Uninstalling the SaveAnything app from your device will permanently and irreversibly delete the local database and all saved vault items from your phone.

8. Feedback

If you contact us or use "Help Us Improve" to send feedback, we receive only what you choose to send, such as your message and email address, and use it only to reply and improve the app.

9. Children's Privacy

SaveAnything is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, data retention, or data deletion practices, do not hesitate to contact us at: support.devduos@thetrishulgroup.com
