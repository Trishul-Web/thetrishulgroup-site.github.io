The short version

  • No account or login is needed to read the news.
  • Your bookmarks, feeds, mixes and settings are stored on your device.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use your advertising ID.
  • Opening a full article takes you to the publisher's website, which has its own privacy policy.

About This Policy

DevDuo's built the Newszio app as an Ad-Supported app. This SERVICE is provided by DevDuos and is intended for use as is.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Newszio uses, where it is stored, which third-party services are involved and the choices you have.

1. Data Storage & Local Preferences

Newszio follows a local-first principle. Your preferences, including bookmarked articles, custom feeds, pinned topics, and country selections, are stored securely on your device using a local database. We (DevDuos) do not transmit or store this personal reading data on our servers.

This also includes your Custom Mixes, saved searches, feed tuning levels, reading mode, narrator settings and any custom RSS feeds you add.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No account: Newszio does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or password.
  • No contacts, camera or microphone: the app does not access them.
  • No reading history on our servers: what you read, save or skip stays on your device.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or provide functionality:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect data such as Advertising ID, coarse location, and usage metrics to serve relevant ads.
  • RSS Feed Providers: Newszio aggregates content from public RSS feeds (e.g., Bing News). When you click to read a full article, you may be directed to the original publisher's website, which may have its own privacy policy and cookie usage.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.

4. News Feeds, Cities and Maps

To load news, the app requests public feeds for the topics, keywords, countries and cities you choose. Like any website, these feed providers receive your IP address and the search terms needed to return results. When you tap a place on the news map or follow a city, only that place name is used to search for news; the app does not need your GPS location for this.

5. Smart Reading Tools & Narrator

When you use summaries, simplify, ask-a-question or perspective tools, the text of the article you are reading may be processed to generate the result. These tools work on the article content and do not require any personal information from you.

The narrator reads articles aloud using the text-to-speech voice on your device.

6. Sharing

When you use Share or create a post image, the app opens your phone's share menu. The content is sent only to the app and people you choose.

7. Log Data

In a case of an error in the app, we collect data and information (through third-party products like Google Play Services) on your phone called Log Data. This may include your device IP address, device name, operating system version, and the time and date of your use of the Service.

8. Ads and Your Choices

  • Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
  • You can pause feed tuning or reset the algorithm at any time inside the app.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your reading data is stored on your device, you control it completely:

  • Remove items: delete saved stories, mixes or feeds inside the app.
  • Clear app data: go to your device's Settings > Apps > Newszio > Storage > Clear storage.
  • Uninstall: uninstalling the app permanently removes all locally stored data.

10. Security

Your preferences are kept in the app's private storage on your device. Connections to news feeds and ad services use encrypted HTTPS where the provider supports it.

11. Children's Privacy

Newszio is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.

13. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support.devduos@thetrishulgroup.com
