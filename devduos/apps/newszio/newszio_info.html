Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open the app and start reading instantly. Your preferences are saved on your device.

Where does the news come from?

Newszio brings together headlines from public news feeds, and every story links back to its original publisher. You can also add your own RSS feeds.

Can I read without internet?

Yes. Save stories and read them later offline from your Library.

I’m a publisher. How do I request a change?

Visit our contact page and send us the article link. We act promptly on content requests.
