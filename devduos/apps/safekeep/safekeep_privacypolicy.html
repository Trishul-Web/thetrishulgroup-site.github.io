About This Policy

DevDuo's ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our Safekeep mobile application, published on Google Play as Safekeep: Secure Folder Vault (the "Service").

1. The “Zero-Knowledge” Principle

Safekeep is a zero-knowledge application. This means we, the developers, have zero access to the files you encrypt or the Master Key you create. Your data's privacy is mathematically guaranteed by the encryption itself, which occurs exclusively on your device.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Your Master Key: Your Master Key is created by you and is never transmitted to our servers. We cannot recover it if you lose it.
  • Your Files: The contents of the files you choose to encrypt are never uploaded or visible to us.
  • Personal Information: We do not require you to create an account with personal details like your name or address to use the core features of the app.
  • Your PINs: your vault, disguise and decoy PINs stay on your device.

3. Information We Collect

Our collection is limited to what is essential for the app's functionality and improvement:

  • Google Account Information (Optional): If you choose to sign in with Google to enable cloud backup, we receive your email address and basic profile information from Google for authentication purposes only. This allows us to connect the Service to your personal Google Drive account.
  • Anonymous Analytics: We may collect anonymous, aggregated usage data, such as app crashes, session duration, and feature usage. This data is not personally identifiable and helps us improve the app's stability and user experience.
  • Purchase Information (Premium Users): If you subscribe to Safekeep Premium, the transaction is handled by the respective app store (Google Play). We receive anonymous purchase tokens to verify your subscription status but do not receive your payment information.

4. How Your Data Is Used

  • Cloud Backup (Optional): If you sign in, the Service will create a private folder named "safekeep-data" in your own Google Drive. Your encrypted files are stored there. We do not have permission to access any other part of your Google Drive.
  • Service Improvement: Anonymous analytics help us identify bugs, understand which features are popular, and make the app better.

5. Device Permissions

  • Photos, videos and files: to let you choose files to encrypt and to save files you decrypt or export.
  • Camera: for Secure Camera, which captures directly into the vault without saving to your gallery, and for Intruder Selfie if you turn it on.
  • Motion sensors: used only for the Panic Gesture, to detect when you flip your phone face down.
  • Internet: for optional Google sign-in and Drive backup, and to verify purchases.

6. Privacy Features and Your Data

  • Intruder Selfie: photos of failed unlock attempts are stored only inside your vault on your device. They are never sent to us.
  • Secure Share: when you share a file as a secure encrypted file, it stays encrypted. The person you send it to needs the key you choose to open it, and decryption happens in their browser or app. We never receive the file or the key.
  • App Masking and Decoy Vault: the calculator, weather or notes disguise and the decoy vault work entirely on your device.
  • Private Notes: notes you write in the vault are encrypted and stored like your files.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, but the ultimate security of your files rests on the strength of your Master Key and the principle of on-device, end-to-end encryption.

Use a strong, unique Master Key and PIN, and keep your phone's screen lock turned on.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Delete inside the app: remove any file, folder, tag or note at any time.
  • Guest accounts: uninstalling the app or clearing its data permanently deletes all files stored on your device.
  • Google backups: you can delete the "safekeep-data" folder from your own Google Drive at any time, and remove Safekeep's access from your Google Account settings.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
