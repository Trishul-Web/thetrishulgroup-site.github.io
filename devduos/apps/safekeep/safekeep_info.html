Questions

Can you see my files?

No. Files are encrypted on your phone with your Master Key, which is never sent to us. We have zero access to your files or your key.

What if I forget my Master Key?

Because of the zero-knowledge design, nobody can recover it, including us. Keep your Master Key written down somewhere safe.

Guest or Google account, which should I use?

Guest keeps everything offline on your phone only. Signing in with Google adds an encrypted backup in a private folder of your own Google Drive, so you can restore it on another device.

Will uninstalling delete my files?

For Guest accounts, yes, everything stored in the app is deleted. Export your files or use a Google backup before uninstalling.
